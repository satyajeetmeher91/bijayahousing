Paste or upload a CSV with a header row. Recognised columns: id, name, category, subcategory, brand, description, imageId, price, cost, stock, sizes, reorder, discount, active. Only name, category, price are required for a new product; include id to update an existing one. Sizes look like S:399:12; M:449:15 (name:price:stock). For several images in imageId, separate them with | (e.g. IMG001|IMG001-2).
